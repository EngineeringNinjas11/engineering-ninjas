Understanding a Single Line Diagram

A beginner-friendly introduction to reading and understanding electrical single line diagrams.

What is an SLD?

A single line diagram is a simplified representation of an electrical power system. It shows the major equipment and electrical connections without drawing every individual conductor.

Why engineers use it

SLDs make complex electrical systems easier to understand, document, review and communicate between engineers, contractors and clients.
